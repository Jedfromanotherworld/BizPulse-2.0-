# BizPulse 2 guide

Owner: Inventory → add products; Receive Stock → choose product and supplier, enter quantity/cost/optional expiry. Existing product stock is changed through Receive Stock. Suppliers → add/edit locations or view the geographic map. Manage → Users, Shifts, QR Settings, Sales Report, transactions, categories and backups. Analytics → offline insights.

Cashier: My Shift → Time In → POS → Scan or Kiosk → add items → checkout Cash or QR → receipt. For QR, verify actual receipt in the receiving payment account before pressing Payment Received. Time Out closes the shift. Owner can force Time Out. Sales are associated with the active shift; totals are derived from the saved receipts.

Backups replace all records after validation and sign out the current user. They include password hashes and images: keep them private. Reset restores demo data and the bundled QR. Uninstalling deletes local data.

Suppliers: records and addresses work offline; map tiles need connectivity. Optional IPv4 is a network address and never determines geographic location. No IP was supplied, so seeded IP fields are blank.
